CHAPTER 01 / 06

First, we reach the institution.

The AptoHire team approaches a university or college. Once verified and onboarded, the institution gets its own workspace and enrolls its students. Its data stays separate from every other institution.

A trusted starting point gives students a path into the ecosystem—and gives the institution visibility into its own talent.

EVIDENCE-LINKED EVALUATION

Every score needs a reason.

  • Video/audio: explanation and communication evidence.
  • Code/repository: implementation and technical execution.
  • Live project/docs: functionality and supporting context.
  • Project-specific rubric: criteria suited to the work.
  • Confidence, evidence gaps and dispute route remain visible.

A coding project should not be scored like a public-speaking contest. The rubric must fit the skill.

Trust is the product

Privacy by design. Consent by default.

ALLOWED

Student-controlled access

  • Student manages own profile and requests.
  • Institution sees its own students and permitted analytics.
  • Organization sees anonymized summaries first.
  • Approved fields are revealed only after consent.
NOT ALLOWED

No open student directory

  • Institution A cannot browse Institution B's private student records.
  • Students cannot browse each other's private profiles.
  • Recruiters cannot bypass consent to reveal identity.
  • AI is decision support, not an unquestionable hiring authority.
